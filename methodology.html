1 · The rules this data follows

  1. Official sources only. A number is used only if it was read on the bank's own domain or a .gov domain during this session. Rate aggregators, news articles and other banks' comparison tables were rejected — including Marcus's and SoFi's tables that quote Chase and Wells Fargo rates.
  2. Blank beats guessed. If a page gates the value behind a ZIP or renders it client-side, the cell stays empty and the item moves to Flagged for review. Seventeen field checks ended that way — most importantly the four ZIP-gated APYs at Chase, Bank of America, Citibank and TD Bank, which are therefore excluded from every ranked table and every projection.
  3. Dates are copied, not assumed. Each APY carries the as-of date printed on the page. Where no date is printed (Barclays Online Savings), the as-of cell is blank and the row is flagged.
  4. Projections are arithmetic, never estimates. Interest is computed from the published APY only.
  5. Every row links to its source. Hover any source link for the full URL; every URL is also listed below.

3 · The formula

ending = principal × (1 + APY/100)years interest = ending − principal. Because APY already reflects compounding, no compounding frequency is applied on top. Tiered accounts use the tier that the deposit falls into, from that bank's own table. Promotional rows are blends of the bank's published base and promotional APYs and are labelled “derived” wherever they appear.

Known limitation: all rates compared here are variable. Holding the APY constant for 1, 3 or 5 years is an assumption made for comparison, not a forecast. No CD is included in this dataset.

7 · How this site is built

Static HTML generated by tools/build_site.py from the JSON in data/v2/: accounts.json, projections.json, promotions.json, banks_fdic.json, verification.json, flagged.json and sources_fetched.json. tools/build_projections.py recomputes every dollar figure and tools/check_links.py verifies that every link is either an official source on file or an existing local page. No CDN, no tracking, no JavaScript required to read any number.

Snapshot: rate and fee data read from bank and FDIC pages on 2026-10-21 / 2026-10-22. Projections assume an account opened 2026-10-22 and a constant APY. Not financial advice.
