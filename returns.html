Notes that change the ranking

  • Axos wins most tiers on paper, but the 4.21% ongoing rate requires monthly activity in an Axos ONE checking account; without it the ongoing rate is 1.00%. The 5.00% intro is applied to the first $50,000 only because Axos does not publish the cap on the page reviewed. See F03/F04.
  • Barclays is modelled on its tier structure: balances below $250,000 earn 3.50%, not the 3.65% headline.
  • CIT pays 0.25% (standard) on balances under $5,000; the promo adds only 0.50%, leaving 0.75% there.
  • SoFi's 4.40% lasts six months and requires an eligible direct deposit; its 4.70% SoFi Plus tier is a separate paid subscription and is not modelled here.
  • Bonuses (up to $800) are excluded from all interest figures and are listed on Promotions.
