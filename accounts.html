Why the large branch-bank rates need extra care

Branch-bank rates are product- and eligibility-specific. Chase shows its savings rate table only after a valid 5-digit ZIP code is entered. Bank of America's page renders fee and minimum-balance values as “$XXXX” to automated readers. Citi's general rate table renders a dynamic placeholder, but the separate Q3 2026 offer terms publish the basic New York tier's 0.98% blended APY for $25,000–$999,999 and 0.03% standard interest rate; the promotion ended for new openings after 2026-10-22. Wells Fargo Platinum Savings separately offers a 3.25% variable rate to eligible new customers in New York with at least $25,000 maintained daily. Its Way2Save 0.01% page rate is only for the default California market (CA 94105), so the Way2Save New York rate remains blank. Chase and Bank of America New York APYs remain blank. See Flagged for review.

Accounts excluded
