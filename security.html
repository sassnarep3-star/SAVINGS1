How the insurance actually stacks

$250,000 per depositor, per bank, per ownership category All of your deposits in the same ownership category at the same bank are added together. Different categories — single, joint, certain retirement accounts, trust — are insured separately. FDIC
$1,250,000 is the trust ceiling Since April 1, 2024, a trust owner with five or more beneficiaries is insured up to $1,250,000 per owner for all trust accounts (POD/ITF, revocable and irrevocable) at the same bank. Naming more beneficiaries does not raise it. FDIC
  1. Check the certificate number, not the logo. Every row above links to its FDIC BankFind record.
  2. Insurance is dollar-for-dollar, including accrued interest. A $250,000 balance that accrues interest above the limit is only insured to the limit — leave headroom or use a joint/trust title.
  3. Use EDIE for your exact situation. edie.fdic.gov is the FDIC's own estimator.
  4. Two FDIC-insured banks are not automatically two coverages if the deposits are held in the same ownership category at the same charter.

Two coverage traps found in this review

SoFi's “up to $3M” is not a SoFi charter limit It is additional coverage through a network of participating banks. SoFi Bank, N.A. itself insures the standard $250,000 per depositor, per bank, per ownership category. sofi.com
Capital One and Discover now count as one bank Capital One's own FAQ: Capital One or Discover Bank deposit accounts opened on or after May 18, 2025 are counted with your other Capital One deposit accounts for FDIC coverage. Splitting between the two brands does not double coverage. capitalone.com
What this page does not prove Being FDIC-insured says nothing about an app's authentication quality. Only Ally publishes an explicit multi-factor-authentication statement and a security guarantee among the pages read here; Capital One publishes a security program page. For the others, app-level security is unverified in this session and listed as such.
