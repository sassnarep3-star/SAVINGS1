What the bonuses are worth in practice

E*TRADE: $800 on $100,000

An $800 bonus on a $100,000 deposit is worth about 0.80% of the deposit in year one. Combined with the 4.25% six-month rate, the first-year value is roughly 5.05% — but the money must stay for the 30-day funding window plus 45 more days, and only new accounts with promo code SAVE800 qualify.

E*TRADE terms

SoFi: $400 on $5,000 of direct deposit

This bonus is tied to payroll or government deposits, not to the size of the savings balance. $400 on $5,000 of deposits is 8%, but it is a one-time payment and requires the direct deposit relationship to be maintained. Bonuses are taxable miscellaneous income.

SoFi terms

Barclays: $200 on $25,000

$200 on $25,000 is 0.80% of the deposit, but the money must stay for 120 days and the offer expires 10/31/2026. It is paid about 60 days after the holding period.

Barclays terms

CIT: BOOST50 is a rate, not cash

The 0.50% APY boost for six months is worth about $12.50 per $5,000, or $250 per $100,000 over the six months, versus the standard tier. It also lifts balances under $5,000 only from 0.25% to 0.75%.
