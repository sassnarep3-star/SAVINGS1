What does “competitive” mean here?

The highest currently published eligible rate in this review is Barclays Select Savings at 4.25% APY for active AARP members (accurate 2026-10-07), with a separate $400 offer that requires a $40,000 balance and a 120-day hold. For products without that membership restriction, Marcus and Barclays Tiered Savings both show 3.50% below $250,000; Barclays Tiered Savings has a $200 limited-time funding bonus. Wells Fargo's 3.25% APY is a conditional new-customer rate. American Express lists 3.20%, Ally 3.10%, and Capital One's 3.20% remains provisional because its own product page rendered “NaN.”

At $235,000 and the current 4.25% AARP rate, one-year gross interest is about $9,988 before fees, taxes, and rate changes. The $400 bonus is excluded from the rate table and has separate membership, funding, and holding rules. Citi's 0.98% blended figure is an expiring one-year offer, not a standard ongoing rate. Chase, Bank of America, and U.S. Bank New York APYs remain blank where location-specific disclosures were not confirmed.

Barclays AARP Select Savings and Barclays Tiered Savings are both at Barclays Bank Delaware and share FDIC insurance aggregation for the same depositor and ownership category. Review the Flagged for Review page before applying.
