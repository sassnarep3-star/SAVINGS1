Bonuses and promotions

One-time cash offers, referral rewards, relationship rates and temporary promotional APYs are different from the ongoing savings rate. The source terms and dates are shown for each item.

How offers affect your comparison: A cash bonus may be taxable and usually requires holding more money than you otherwise would. Any AARP membership dues are excluded from the Barclays Select Savings return scenario. A temporary rate boost or promotional APY is not a year-round fixed rate. The return tables exclude cash bonuses; count one only if you meet every requirement and the bank confirms your eligibility. Citi's promotional opening deadline is the snapshot date, October 22, 2026; its 0.98% figure is a one-year blended APY, not an ongoing standard rate.
