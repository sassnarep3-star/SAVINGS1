Patterns worth knowing

Branch banks charge a fee and waive it with a relationship Chase $5 (waived at $300), Wells Fargo $5 (waived at $300), PNC $5 (waived at $300 or a linked checking account), U.S. Bank $5, Citi $4.50 (waived at $500), TD $5/$15. The cheapest way to hold these accounts is usually to link a checking account — which is also how the banks earn the relationship.
Online banks charge nothing and have no minimum Ally, Marcus, Amex, Capital One, Synchrony, Barclays and SoFi all state no monthly fee and no minimum balance. Their limits are different: Ally caps withdrawals at 10 per statement cycle, Barclays caps the account at $1,000,000, and Marcus caps an Online Savings account at $1,000,000.
Funding windows are real Capital One gives 60 days to fund after approval; TD closes the account automatically if no deposit is made within 45 days; Marcus returns balances above its limits.
Four banks' opening requirements could not be fully read Chase, Bank of America, Citibank and TD have components (rates, fees or minimums) that render client-side. See Flagged for review.
