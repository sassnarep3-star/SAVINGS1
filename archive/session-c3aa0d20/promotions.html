Two things to check before chasing a bonus

A bonus is a one-time payment, a rate is every year $200 on $25,000 is 0.80% once. A 3.50% account pays $8,750 a year on $250,000. If a bonus account pays 0.05%, the bonus is wiped out by the rate gap in a few months.
Bonuses usually require new money and a hold period Barclays: new Tiered Savings customers only, $25,000 in within 30 days and kept for 120 consecutive days, paid about 60 days later, and reported to the IRS as interest. Read the full terms on the bank's page.
