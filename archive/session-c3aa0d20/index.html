Read before acting
  • Every rate here is variable and can change before or after the account is opened. The projections hold the rate constant only to make the comparison possible.
  • The projections are arithmetic on published APYs, not bank quotes. Interest is taxable and is reported on Form 1099-INT.
  • Branch-bank rate sheets are ZIP-gated. U.S. Bank was read at ZIP 55402, PNC at ZIP 15222 and Wells Fargo at CA 94105 — flagged.
  • FDIC coverage is $250,000 per depositor, per bank, per ownership category. Splitting cash across ownership categories or banks — not picking a famous brand — is what raises coverage.
