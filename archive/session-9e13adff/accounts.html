SCOPE NOTE

Large national providers

This dashboard covers consumer deposit savings products at Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo, Capital One and Citi. Account pages confirmed mobile/digital banking and describe accounts as FDIC insured, with general coverage rules detailed on the security page.

It does not attempt to represent every national provider, credit union, online-only bank, brokerage sweep, CD, or money-market mutual fund. No third-party APY data is substituted when a bank's own current rate is unavailable.
