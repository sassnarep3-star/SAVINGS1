Barclays Bank Delaware — Select Savings from Barclays (AARP Digital Banking) · 4.25%

Why it is here: NEW — the highest flat, unconditional-on-balance APY found in this review. Carried by neither published dataset.

Rate scope: 4.25% on EVERY balance tier: <$10,000 · $10,000–$24,999.99 · $25,000–$49,999.99 · $50,000–$99,999.99 · $100,000–$249,999.99 · $250,000+. No balance condition and no intro clock.

Effective date: APY on all balance tiers is accurate as of 10/07/2026

Fee waiver: No monthly maintenance fees

Compounding: Compounded daily, credited monthly

Opening bonus: $400 — open by 10/31/2026, fund $40,000+ within 30 days, hold $40,000+ for 120 consecutive days; bonus added about 60 days later and reported on Form 1099-INT

Eligibility: Requires a current, active AARP membership number at application. AARP is not an FDIC-insured institution; the bank is Barclays Bank Delaware, Member FDIC.

Balance / account limits: Maximum $1,000,000 per Select Savings account; maximum 3 Select Savings accounts per customer; 25 total Barclays deposit accounts; up to 4 beneficiaries per account

Mobile app: Barclays US App (iOS 15+ / Android 12+); mobile check deposit

Security: Password protection, internet firewalls and encryption on the website and app; one-time passcodes for password reset; device access can be revoked by phone

Barclays Bank Delaware — Online Savings · 3.20%

Why it is here: NEW — Barclays' non-tiered product; the live dashboards carried only Tiered Savings.

Rate scope: Same APY regardless of balance (this is what distinguishes it from Tiered Savings)

Effective date: Read on the official product page 2026-10-22; the page prints no separate effective date for this product

Fee waiver: No monthly maintenance fees; no minimum balance to open

Compounding: Compounded daily, credited monthly

Withdrawal rules: No limit on the number or type of withdrawals and transfers per month (stated in the product FAQ)

Opening bonus: none found on the official page reviewed

Mobile app: Barclays US App

Security: Password protection, firewalls, encryption; 5-business-day hold on check/electronic deposits (funds on hold still earn interest)

BMO Bank N.A. (Alto, formerly BMO Alto) — Alto Online Savings Account · 3.00%

Why it is here: NEW — carried by neither published dataset.

Rate scope: All balances; no tiers

Effective date: APY as of October 21, 2026 (printed on the product page)

Fee waiver: No fees; no minimum balance. Must be funded within 90 days or the account is closed; a zero balance for 90 consecutive days closes the account.

Compounding: Variable rate; may change at any time after the account is opened

Opening bonus: none found on the official page reviewed

Mobile app: not verified — Alto states accounts cannot be accessed through BMO Digital Banking, BMO branches or BMO ATMs; servicing is via Alto Online Banking. No separate Alto mobile app was named on the official page.

Security: not verified

FDIC aggregation warning: Deposits made through Alto and in other U.S. accounts under the BMO brand are NOT separately insured — they count toward one FDIC limit at BMO Bank N.A. (Cert #16571).

Truist Bank — Truist One Savings · 0.01%

Why it is here: NEW — the only top-10-by-assets national bank absent from both published datasets. Its rate IS published, dated and un-gated.

Rate scope: Every tier from $0–$4,999.99 up to $5,000,000+ pays 0.01%. Truist states tiered interest is NOT applicable to this account.

Effective date: Interest rates and APY accurate as of 9/28/2026

Fee waiver: $0 with ANY ONE of: a related Truist personal checking account; a $300+ minimum daily ledger balance; a recurring preauthorized internal transfer of $25+ per statement cycle; or an owner under 18 (must open in branch)

Compounding: Calculated and compounded daily on the collected balance, credited monthly

U.S. Bank National Association — Elite Money Market — new-client rate · 4.00%

Why it is here: NEW — the live dashboards left U.S. Bank's savings APY blank. This separate U.S. Bank product publishes a dated new-client rate plus its full standard tier ladder without a ZIP gate.

Rate scope: Up to 4.00% APY for new Elite Money Market clients who deposit at least $25,000 within 30 days of opening and keep a $25,000+ minimum daily balance. Standard tiers otherwise: under $10,000 → 0.01%; $10,000–$24,999.99 → 0.01%; $25,000–$49,999.99 → 0.05%; $50,000–$99,999.99 → 0.05%.

Effective date: Read on the official product page 2026-10-20 / 2026-10-21; the page states APYs may vary by location

Fee waiver: $0 with a $10,000 minimum daily balance, OR as an owner of a U.S. Bank Smartly Checking account; also waived on all new Elite Money Market accounts for twelve months from opening

Compounding: Compounded daily, credited monthly

Opening bonus: none found on the official page reviewed

Eligibility: Rate is for NEW Elite Money Market clients only and is not valid for clients with an existing U.S. Bank savings or money market account. Both new and existing accounts open fewer than 30 days qualify with a $25,000+ balance.

Mobile app: U.S. Bank Mobile App

Security: not verified

Product caveat: This is a money market deposit account, not a statement savings account. It is FDIC-insured on the same terms, but it is a different product type from the savings accounts elsewhere in this comparison.

First-Citizens Bank & Trust Company (CIT Bank division) — Savings Connect · 3.65%

Why it is here: NEW — CIT's flat-rate account. The live dashboards carried only the tiered Platinum Savings, which pays just 0.25% below $5,000.

Rate scope: 3.65% on the full balance with no tiers and no qualifying-balance requirement

Effective date: APY accurate as of July 1, 2026 (the date printed in CIT's footnote)

Fee waiver: No account opening or monthly maintenance fees

Compounding: Compounded daily, credited monthly

Opening bonus: none found on the official page reviewed

Mobile app: CIT Bank mobile app with mobile check deposit and transfers

Security: Continuous fraud monitoring (stated on CIT's own Platinum Savings boost page)

FDIC aggregation warning: CIT Bank is a division of First-Citizens Bank & Trust Company. Deposits held under First-Citizens and all of its divisions are NOT separately insured — they must be combined against one $250,000 limit (Cert #11063).
