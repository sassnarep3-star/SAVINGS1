This is the third dashboard in this repository. The main dashboard ranks 18 products and the cross-check dashboard independently re-verifies 20. This layer does something different: it goes back to the items those two left blank and resolves them from official regional rate-sheet PDFs and fee schedules, then adds the nationally-available accounts neither dataset carried.

Returns over 1, 2, 3 and 5 years

Interest at seven deposit sizes for every account in this layer with a verified APY — plus the FDIC safe-harbour arithmetic that a flat $250,000 deposit quietly breaks.

Assumptions, stated once and applied everywhere. Lump sum deposited 2026-10-22. No additions, no withdrawals, no fees deducted (every account projected here has a $0 monthly fee or a waiver the deposit itself satisfies). Interest compounds at the bank's published APY, held constant for the whole horizon. Cash bonuses are excluded — they are listed on the account pages, never folded into an interest figure. Every APY here is variable and the bank can change it the day after the account is opened, so the multi-year columns are illustrations of a constant-rate scenario, not forecasts.

Which accounts are projected — and which are deliberately not

Projected

The 10 accounts in this layer with a verified APY read from an official page. Each pays its rate on the whole balance, so the arithmetic is unambiguous.

Not projected

Citi Savings and PNC High Yield Savings have no readable or no unambiguous APY and are excluded entirely rather than estimated. Axos ONE is not projected in this layer because its headline and its own rate table disagree — see flag S01. Conditional rates (Wells Fargo $25,000+, U.S. Bank Elite Money Market $25,000+ for new clients) are projected only at deposit sizes where their condition is met; smaller sizes are shown for completeness but the condition is noted on the account page.

Figures in amber are on balances above $250,000 — the FDIC single-owner limit at one bank. That portion is uninsured unless it is spread across banks or ownership categories. See Max FDIC coverage.

The FDIC safe-harbour problem nobody's table shows

If you deposit exactly $250,000 to hit the FDIC limit, you are uninsured by the end of the first month — because the interest pushes the balance above the ceiling, and FDIC coverage includes principal and accrued interest up to the limit. At Barclays Bank Delaware's 4.25%, the largest opening deposit that stays inside $250,000 for a full year is $239,808.15. Deposit more than that and the excess is uninsured for as long as it sits there.

Three ways to stay fully insured while still earning

  • Open below the line. Deposit $239,808.15 rather than $250,000 and let a year of interest bring the balance to the ceiling.
  • Sweep the interest out. Move credited interest to a second insured bank monthly or quarterly; the principal then stays at exactly $250,000 indefinitely.
  • Use a second ownership category. A joint account or a trust account at the same bank is insured separately from a single account — see Max FDIC coverage for the exact ceilings.
