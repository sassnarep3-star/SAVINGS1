low S08 — The FDIC trust category raises the single-bank ceiling to $1,250,000 — a fact absent from the published coverage tables
Since April 1, 2024 the FDIC insures trust accounts at $250,000 per eligible beneficiary up to five beneficiaries, for a maximum of $1,250,000 per owner per insured bank, covering both revocable (POD/ITF) and irrevocable trusts. The live dashboards' coverage tables show only the $250,000 single and $500,000 joint figures, which understates what one bank can insure for a depositor who uses the trust category.
 www.fdic.gov needs review

How a blank is treated here

A blank cell renders as — and means the value could not be read from an official page on 2026-10-22. It never means zero and never means "none". Two values in this layer are deliberately left blank despite being partially visible: Citibank's standard savings APY (injected at runtime) and PNC's High Yield Savings APY (two official PNC URLs disagree). Neither was resolved to a number, because resolving them would require choosing between conflicting official figures.
