What this layer found

The Big Four rates were never actually unpublished

Both live dashboards report that Chase, Bank of America, Citi and Wells Fargo savings APYs "could not be verified" because the websites gate them behind a ZIP code or render them client-side. That is true of the web pages — but three of these four banks publish the same rates as dated, market-specific PDF rate sheets on their own domains, which render perfectly. Chase New York: 0.01%. Bank of America New York–Tri-State: 0.04%. Wells Fargo Platinum Savings new-customer rate: 3.25% on $25,000+.

Chase NY rate sheet · BofA NY–Tri-State rate sheet · Wells Fargo addenda

The headline that does not appear in its own rate table

Axos advertises "up to 5.00% APY" and the main dashboard ranks it first on that basis. The rate table printed lower on the same Axos page shows the boosted rate for every tier from $0 to $249,999.99 as 4.21% APY, and 3.50% from $250,000 up. No tier pays 5.00%. Published here as flag S01 rather than silently corrected.

Axos ONE page

A 4.25% flat rate with no tier, no clock and no checking bundle

Select Savings from Barclays pays 4.25% APY on every balance tier — not an introductory rate, not conditional on a linked checking account, not conditional on a balance threshold. It requires an active AARP membership number at application. A $400 bonus is attached to a $40,000 deposit held 120 days, but the offer closes 10/31/2026.

Barclays Select Savings

One bank can insure $1,250,000 — not $250,000

Both live dashboards' coverage tables stop at the $250,000 single-owner and $500,000 joint figures. Since 1 April 2024 the FDIC insures trust accounts at $250,000 per eligible beneficiary up to five beneficiaries, for $1,250,000 per owner, per insured bank. For a max-coverage goal that changes the arithmetic materially — see Max FDIC coverage.

Items closed against a primary source

R01 — Big Four savings APYs could not be verified from official pages (published as high severity on the live site)

Three of the four are now verified from the banks' own dated, market-correct primary documents: Chase Savings 0.01% (New York PDF rate sheet, in effect 2026-10-16), Bank of America Advantage Savings 0.04% (New York – Tri-State rate sheet, effective 2026-10-21), and Wells Fargo Platinum Savings 3.25% for new savings customers with $25,000+ (Consumer Account Addenda, effective 2026-10-07). Only Citi's standard savings APY remains unreadable.

www.chase.com · media.bac-assets.com · www.wellsfargo.com

R02 — Bank of America Advantage Savings fee and minimum balance could not be read ('$XXXX' placeholders)

Both are published in Bank of America's Personal Schedule of Fees, effective August 7, 2026: monthly maintenance fee $8.00, minimum amount to open $100.00, with the fee waived for the first six months from account opening for new accounts.

www.bankofamerica.com

R03 — Mobile app existence not confirmed for Ally, Marcus, Barclays or Wells Fargo

All four are confirmed on the banks' own pages. Ally Mobile app with biometric verification and Ally eCheck Deposit; Marcus mobile banking app with fingerprint/face login and 128-bit SSL; Barclays US App (iOS 15+/Android 12+) with mobile check deposit; Wells Fargo Mobile® app with an in-app Security Center.

www.ally.com · www.marcus.com · banking.us.barclays · www.wellsfargo.com

R04 — Security controls could not be verified for Marcus by Goldman Sachs and Wells Fargo

Both publish security pages describing account controls. Marcus: multi-factor authentication, SSL/EV SSL encryption, firewalls, around-the-clock network monitoring, biometric app login. Wells Fargo: passkeys, biometric sign-on, voice verification, two-step verification, account alerts and automatic sign-out.

www.marcus.com · www.wellsfargo.com
