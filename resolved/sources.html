This is the third dashboard in this repository. The main dashboard ranks 18 products and the cross-check dashboard independently re-verifies 20. How to re-check any figure

Open the linked document and search for the account name. Rate-sheet PDFs print their own effective date at the top — Chase's New York sheet says "RATES IN EFFECT AS OF: Friday, October 16, 2026", Bank of America's New York–Tri-State sheet says "Effective as of: October 21, 2026", and Wells Fargo's addenda says "Platinum Savings Rate Update, Effective October 7, 2026". Those printed dates are what appear in the tables on this site; where a page prints no date, the table says so explicitly instead of substituting the date it was read.
