This section keeps the independent 20-account dataset, its 41 registered sources, the tier-aware return maths and the 26-item flagged list, so the two can be compared line by line. Where the two disagree, both figures are published and the difference is explained on Flagged for review.

Status key: unverified = page did not reveal the value; irregular = conflicting or broken official output; scope = tied to a ZIP/tier/date; risk = a stated condition that cuts the rate; note = important context.

How to close these out

  1. Open the official rate page for Chase, Bank of America, Citibank or TD with ZIP 10001 in a normal browser; the rate table only appears after the ZIP is accepted.
  2. For Synchrony, confirm at application whether 3.55% or 2.85% applies to your account.
  3. For Barclays, confirm a mobile app exists if that is a hard requirement.
  4. For Axos, decide whether you can keep the checking bundle alive every month — missing it drops the savings rate to 1.00%, which is worse than an ordinary high-yield account.
  5. For U.S. Bank, PNC and Wells Fargo, re-read the rate table with a New York ZIP before comparing.
