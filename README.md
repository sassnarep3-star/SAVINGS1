# Savings account research dashboard

A source-linked comparison of savings accounts offered by large U.S. banks, prepared for a New York City consumer. Research snapshot and prospective opening date: **October 22, 2026**.

> **Early finding:** Among the accounts and current public rate information verified here, Wells Fargo Platinum Savings advertises the highest continuing variable APY found (3.25%) for qualifying new Wells Fargo savings customers who hold at least $25,000. Capital One 360 Performance Savings is close at 3.20%, with no minimum or monthly fee. Neither rate is locked. Citi's public savings-rate promotion is available only through October 22, 2026, and its official New York APY table is dated July 14, 2026; see the qualifications and review flags before relying on it. This is a comparison of the named large national banks—not a claim that these are the highest savings rates available from every U.S. institution.
