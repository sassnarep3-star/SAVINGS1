BEST VERIFIED RATE IN SCOPE

Wells Fargo Platinum Savings

3.25% APY

Highest verified continuing rate in this comparison, if eligible for the new-savings-customer offer and able to keep at least $25,000 in the account. Opening deposit is $25, but that alone does not qualify for the 3.25% APY. The rate is variable, not a fixed-term return.

  • Current qualifying balance: $25,000 minimum, reached within 30 days
  • New Wells Fargo savings customers only; no consumer savings account relationship in the prior 30 days
  • $12 monthly fee; waived with $3,500 minimum daily balance during each fee period

WHAT MATTERS MOST

Three decisions before opening

Read how we verified the data ↗
01

Qualification beats headline rate

Wells Fargo's rate requires a $25,000 balance and customer eligibility. Citi's higher-looking 3.75% is a short-duration promotional interest rate, not a 3.75% APY.

02

Interest counts toward FDIC coverage

The $250,000 limit includes principal and accrued interest. A $250,000 opening deposit can exceed the limit as interest posts. The returns page shows a one-year theoretical principal ceiling at today's top APY.

03

Variable rates don't lock a lifespan

Savings accounts can change rates after opening. Multi-year returns are scenarios, not promises. For a fixed rate and fixed term, compare CDs separately.
