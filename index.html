What the data says

1. The best headline rate is conditional, not automatic

Axos advertises 5.00% APY for six months, but only inside an Axos ONE checking bundle with monthly direct deposits and an average daily balance. Miss a month and the standard tier is 1.00%. The 5.00% is also 1.00 percentage points above the top of the Federal Reserve's target range — a strong signal it is temporary.

source

2. No-strings rates cluster at 3.10%–4.20%

EverBank pays 4.20% with $0 to open and no monthly fee; Openbank pays 4.00% after its six-month boost; Synchrony 3.55%; Marcus 3.50%. These are the cleanest comparisons because the rate does not depend on a second account.

EverBank rates · Openbank

3. Big Four APYs are not publicly checkable

Chase requires a ZIP code, Bank of America renders placeholders, Citi injects the rate at runtime, and Wells Fargo's page showed 0.01% only for its default California market. None is verified for New York, so all four are blank here rather than quoted from third parties. Their fees are visible: $4.50–$5.00 per month unless a balance condition is met.

How to read this dashboard

The question this answers

Given a deposit of $1,000 to $1,000,000, opened on 2026-10-22, which national FDIC-insured bank with a digital app pays the most — and how much of that return depends on a temporary promotion, a second account, or monthly activity?

Return tables show the blended first year, the steady-state year, and a five-year projection at constant rates. Bonuses are listed separately and never folded into the interest math.

The rules applied

  • Only official sources: bank product pages, FDIC BankFind API, FDIC national rate table, Federal Reserve.
  • No aggregator or review site is used as the source for any value.
  • If a field could not be read from an official page, it is blank and flagged.
  • Every table row carries a link to the official page for manual review.
  • FDIC status is checked by certificate number, not by a logo on the page.
