Don't overfund the FDIC limit

FDIC coverage is based on all deposits at the same insured bank and ownership category—not on each separate account. The standard limit includes accrued interest.

At an unchanged 4.25% APY, a $240,000 principal would grow to about $250,200 after one year, above the standard single-owner limit. The dashboard uses $235,000 as a more conservative illustrative deposit; other deposits at the bank or a rate change may still reduce headroom.

Barclays Tiered Savings and the AARP-member Select Savings are both held at Barclays Bank Delaware, so those balances aggregate for FDIC coverage in the same ownership category.
