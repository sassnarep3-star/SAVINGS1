How unverified values are shown

In the tables, an unverified value appears as — in red, never as an estimate. A blank means “this could not be confirmed from an official source on 2026-10-22”, not zero and not “none”. The most consequential blanks are the four Big Four APYs, which are excluded from every return calculation rather than sourced from a comparison site.
