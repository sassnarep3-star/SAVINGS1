How this was built and verified

Scope, formulas, the verification standard applied to each field, and the limits of the analysis.

Reproducible by design. The site is generated from three JSON files by a Python script; every rate carries its source URL and the date it was read.

Scope

  • Included: nationally available savings accounts at FDIC-insured banks with a mobile app or online banking, including national branch banks and national digital banks.
  • Excluded: credit unions, local/regional banks, accounts not open to new customers, brokered deposits, CDs (except where a bank's own page is used for context), and non-deposit products.
  • Reference date: all rates and terms read on 2026-10-22. Account opening date used in every calculation: 2026-10-22.

Calculation

Interest is compounded at the stated APY. For accounts with an introductory rate, the intro rate is applied for exactly the advertised number of months, then the balance is re-tiered at the bank's published post-intro rate for the remaining period. Tiered accounts are modelled either marginally (each band applies to its portion) or as a cliff (the whole balance earns the rate of the band it falls into), according to how the bank describes the account. Bonuses are excluded. No fees are deducted because every digital account in the set has a $0 monthly fee; the Big Four's fees are shown but their APYs are unverified, so they cannot be modelled.

Formulas: balance = Σ portion × (1 + rate)^years; blended first-year APY = (balance₁ / deposit) − 1; five-year projection applies the post-intro rate for the remaining 4.5 years.

Limitations

  • APYs are variable. A rate verified today can change tomorrow; only E*TRADE's six-month boost is described as guaranteed for its term.
  • Bank pages sometimes render rates with JavaScript or behind a ZIP gate. Those values are blank here, not approximated.
  • Tier structures differ between banks. The model follows each bank's own description; where two official pages conflict, the more conservative reading is used and the conflict is flagged.
  • Bonuses are one-time and taxable; they are not annualised into any APY.
  • This is research, not financial, tax, or legal advice.
