Opening rules & offers

Read the qualification requirements before funding. Opening minimums are not necessarily the minimum needed to earn the advertised rate.

Research date: October 22, 2026

TOP RATE QUALIFICATION

Wells Fargo Platinum Savings

3.25% APY¹

Rate requirements

  • $25 minimum opening deposit. The online applicant must be 18 or older; Wells Fargo lists required identity and address details in the linked opener information.
  • $25,000 qualifying balance. Put at least $25,000 in the new account within 30 days and maintain at least that collected balance each day to earn the advertised APY.
  • New savings customers only. Primary owner cannot have been an owner, joint owner, trustee, or a trust on any Wells Fargo consumer savings account within the preceding 30 days.
  • Variable rate. Rate may change; if the balance falls below $25,000, the standard or relationship rate applies for that day. Current standard rate at lower balances is 0.01% APY.

Official evidence: Wells Fargo current rates and qualifying-rate terms ↗; identity / opening rules ↗.

Fees, offer type

$12 monthly service fee. Avoid it with a $3,500 minimum daily balance each fee period. The 3.25% is an APY offer, not a cash bonus and not a guaranteed fixed-term rate.

Official rate page marks 3.25% APY accurate as of October 7, 2026. The offer page reviewed does not state a fixed expiration date. Re-check the bank's rate on application day.

NO-MINIMUM ALTERNATIVE

Capital One 360 Performance Savings

3.20% APY²

Who can open / how to fund

  • Capital One's agreement says the applicant must be a U.S. citizen or lawful permanent resident with a U.S. physical address in the 50 states or Washington, D.C. (including listed military addresses), age 18+, and have a U.S.-based mobile number and SSN or ITIN.
  • No minimum deposit is required. The account is considered opened after funding with at least one cent.
  • Opening can be online, on mobile, or at a Capital One location; the bank says applicants may need to provide personal and employment information and can be denied.
  • Variable APY applies to all balances; interest compounds and is credited monthly per the agreement.

Official evidence: account agreement / opening requirements ↗; product page ↗.

Fees, offer type

$0 monthly maintenance fee and no minimum to open or maintain. No savings-specific cash bonus is listed on the official product or rates pages reviewed.

The official account comparison lists 3.20% APY effective October 21, 2026. The product page currently displays “NaN” in its rate field; see Flagged for Review.

TIME-LIMITED PUBLIC OFFER · EXPIRES TODAY

Citi Savings promotional rate

Through Oct 22, 2026

Offer details as posted

  • The official Citi offer page states new Citi Savings accounts opened July 14 through October 22, 2026 may qualify for a 3.75% promotional interest rate for up to three months. This is the stated interest rate, not a 3.75% APY.
  • At least $25,000 in New-to-Citibank Funds is required to earn the applicable rate. The terms use an on-deposit balance check on the 11th business day after opening.
  • Promotion ends at the earliest of the promotional period, $10,000 maximum interest earned, or—after the first 10 business days—the on-deposit balance falling below the required minimum.
  • New York-region offer APY chart (dated July 14, 2026) lists 0.98% for regular Citi Savings, 1.00% Citi Priority, 1.02% Citigold, and 1.05% Citigold Private Client for qualifying balances from $25,000 to $999,999.99. These annualized APYs assume the promo expires and the stated standard rate remains unchanged; the source is stale versus October 22.

Official terms and New York APY chart: Citi Q3 2026 offer disclosure ↗.

Fees and decision points

Citi states a $4.50 monthly savings fee; waiver with a $500+ average monthly balance, ownership of a Citi checking account, or eligible Relationship Tier. The savings product itself has no minimum opening deposit. Recheck the live offer and final rate sheet before applying; the offer is at its posted end date and no post-October 22 terms are verified here.

This is an interest-rate promotion, not a cash bonus. A related checking-account cash bonus is separate and is not counted as a savings bonus in this comparison.
